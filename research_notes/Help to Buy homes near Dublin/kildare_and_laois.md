# New-build 3-bed houses eligible for Help to Buy in County Kildare and east Laois (research notes, 8 November 2026)

Scope: new-build 3-bedroom HOUSES (duplexes/apartments noted separately) at or under the €500,000 Help to Buy (HTB) ceiling, purchasable by 31 December 2027, in Maynooth, Leixlip, Celbridge, Kilcock, Clane, Prosperous, Sallins, Naas, Newbridge, Kildare town, Monasterevin, Athy, Rathangan, Kilcullen, Portarlington, Portlaoise and Killenard. All prices are asking prices with the listing/article date noted; anything dated 2025 or undated is flagged as possibly stale. Daft.ie and MyHome listing pages were fetched directly where possible; several developer/agent pages (Cairn, Lisney, Knight Frank, Leinster Express, gov.ie) returned 403/503 and could not be read, which is noted in Gaps. The web-search budget for this session was exhausted before every town could be re-queried, so some towns (Sallins, Kilcullen, Rathangan) rely on the county-level Daft listing page rather than per-scheme fetches.

## Scheme context: Help to Buy, First Home Scheme and the "Starter Home Purchase Scheme"

### Takeaway
HTB (max relief now €35,000, price cap still €500,000) runs to end-2029 so any purchase closing by end-2027 is inside the window; the First Home Scheme (FHS) cap in Co Kildare is €475,000. The "Starter Home Purchase Scheme" (SHPS) is simply the Government's 2026 rebrand of the Local Authority Affordable Purchase Scheme (council/LDA shared-equity discount homes); HTB can be stacked on it, FHS cannot.

### Cited Findings
- HTB was extended to 31 December 2029 in Budget 2026 (October 2025); the €500,000 price threshold was not raised, which Savills called a "missed opportunity" — [Crowe Budget 2026 highlights](https://www.crowe.com/ie/insights/budget-2026-highlights); [Irish Times Budget 2026 Q&A](https://www.irishtimes.com/your-money/2025/10/06/your-budget-2026-questions-answered)
- Budget 2027 (October 2026) raised the maximum HTB relief from €30,000 to €35,000, "other parameters remain unchanged"; sources conflict on whether the effective date is 7 October 2026 or 7 October 2027 — [Chartered Accountants Ireland Budget 2027](https://www.charteredaccountants.ie/budget-updates/budget-2027/news/budget-2027-housing-and-property-measures); [Irish Examiner](https://www.irishexaminer.com/news/politics/arid-+41920831.html)
- Lisney argues the €500,000 HTB upper limit "is no longer adequate" — [Lisney](https://lisney.com/?p=105870)
- The First Home shared-equity scheme price ceiling in Co Kildare is €475,000 (Irish Times, Sept 2025, describing Stradowe Meadows as inside both HTB and FHS limits) — [Irish Times, 25 Sept 2025](https://www.irishtimes.com/property/residential/2025/09/25/three-and-four-bed-homes-from-390000-qualify-for-first-time-buyer-schemes-in-athy-co-kildare)
- SHPS definition: "Delivered by local authorities and the Land Development Agency (LDA). Offers new, high-quality, starter-sized homes at reduced prices through shared equity"; it was "previously the Local Authority Affordable Purchase Scheme" — [gov.ie Starter Homes for purchasers](https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-housing-local-government-and-heritage/publications/starter-homes-for-purchasers) (page itself returned 403 on fetch; wording from search extract); [SDCC Starter Homes programme](https://sdcc.ie/en/services/housing/starter-homes-programme/)
- Laois County Council's page is now headed "Starter Homes Programme" and refers to "Starter Home Purchase Schemes (Previously Local Authority Affordable Purchase Schemes)" — [laois.ie](https://laois.ie/housing/starter-homes-programme/schemes-currently-open-for-applications)
- Mechanics (Glenveagh FAQ): the local authority contributes 5%–40% of the price for the same % equity; buyer must be a first-time buyer or Fresh Start, have purchasing power of no more than 95% of the price, a 10% deposit, and must buy out the equity within 40 years; HTB (up to €35,000 on a home up to €500,000) "can be used alongside the Starter Home Purchase Scheme" — [Glenveagh SHPS FAQs](https://glenveagh.ie/news/starter-home-purchase-scheme-faqs)
- Meath County Council FAQ: "Help to Buy can be used in conjunction with the Starter Home Purchase Scheme... you cannot use the Starter Home Purchase Scheme and the First Homes Scheme together" — [Meath CoCo SHPS FAQ](https://www.meath.ie/council/council-services/housing/starter-home-purchase-scheme/starter-home-purchase-scheme-frequently-asked-questions) (from search extract; page returned 503 on direct fetch)
- Each SHPS offer needs its own application via the council's portal; "there is no general application form" — [Meath CoCo SHPS FAQ](https://www.meath.ie/council/council-services/housing/starter-home-purchase-scheme/starter-home-purchase-scheme-frequently-asked-questions)
- The LDA's Barracksfield West project page states that homes in the first two phases (66 + 25) "were offered through this scheme" (the Starter Home Purchase Scheme) — [LDA Barracksfield West project page](https://lda.ie/projects/barracksfield-west)
- Kildare County Council's Barracksfield Phase 2A page calls it an "Affordable Housing Scheme (affordable dwelling purchase arrangements under the Affordable Housing Act 2021)"; the council takes an equity share equal to the discount, will not seek to realise it for 40 years, and priority is 70% by time of complete application / 30% to 5-year Kildare residents — [KCC Barracksfield West Phase 2A](https://kildarecoco.ie/AllServices/Housing/AffordableHousing/BarracksfieldWestPhase2Naas/)

### Inferences
- "Starter Home Purchase Scheme" and "Local Authority Affordable Purchase Scheme" are the same product; news reports in 2025–mid-2026 use the old name, the Irish Times September 2026 guide and the LDA project page use the new name.
- Because HTB stacks with SHPS but FHS does not, a buyer at Barracksfield West can use HTB (now up to €35,000) plus the council equity but must choose between SHPS and FHS.

### Gaps
- Could not read gov.ie's SHPS page directly (403) to confirm the exact date of the rename or any new national income limits; council-level pages (e.g. KCC Phase 2A) set per-unit income caps.
- Whether the €35,000 HTB increase applies from 7 October 2026 or 2027 is reported inconsistently; the official Revenue page was not fetched.

## Key question 2: Door-to-door commute to Dublin city centre by rail, bus and car

### Takeaway
Published station-to-station times are roughly: Maynooth–Connolly 30–50 min (every ~30 min, several per hour at peak), Sallins–Heuston ~33–50 min, Athy–Heuston ~50 min (almost hourly), Portlaoise–Heuston ~1 hour; Newbridge, Kildare, Sallins and Kilcock are inside the Dublin Commuter Zone (Leap singles €3.90–€7.50 since April 2025) while Monasterevin, Portarlington, Portlaoise and Athy are outside it. Irish Rail says peak frequency "cannot currently be increased" and an NTA determination proposes ~15% average fare rises during 2027. Official timetable PDFs could not be fetched, so most journey times are from agents' articles or aggregator sites and should be checked on irishrail.ie.

## Key question 3: DART+ West to Maynooth/Kilcock and other infrastructure affecting value

### Takeaway
DART+ West has its Railway Order (approved July 2024, operative June 2025 after judicial reviews were settled), CPOs have begun and "early enabling works are expected to commence in 2026", but no main contractor is appointed and no opening date is published; the extension to Kilcock with a depot west of Kilcock (target 2031) was confirmed in March 2026. DART trains will not be running to Maynooth or Kilcock before a purchase completing by end-2027; the realistic service date is around the end of the decade at the earliest.

### Cited Findings
- An Bord Pleanála approved the DART+ West Railway Order on 18 July 2024 (board vote 4–2), refusing the depot west of Maynooth on flood-risk grounds — [Newstalk](https://newstalk.com/news/dart-extension-to-maynooth-gets-green-light-from-an-bord-pleanala-1750467); [ABP board record](https://www.pleanala.ie/anbordpleanala/media/abp/cases/bmr/314/b314232.pdf)

## Key question 4: Property Price Register medians for new 3-beds and the new-build vs resale gap

### Takeaway
No source publishes a town-level median for new 3-beds; CSO 12-month medians (all dwellings, to Dec 2025) run from €340,000 (Athy) and €405,950 (Monasterevin) through €420,000 (Kildare town), €435,000 (Naas), €440,000 (Newbridge) to €484,999 (Celbridge), with Kildare county at €447,876 (to May 2026). PPR entries for new builds (Stradowe €365,639; Meadow Mill €326k–€374k; Coill an Easpaig €295k–€357k; An Lochán €370k–€379k; Millers Hill €343,612) are registered net of 13.5% VAT and so sit 10–15% below asking; new-build asking prices generally exceed the town resale median by €0–€80k in the south/west towns and are far above it in north Kildare.

### Cited Findings
- CSO medians for the 12 months to December 2025 (reported 18 Feb 2026): Celbridge €484,999; Newbridge €440,000; Naas €435,000; Curragh €428,505; Kildare €420,000; Monasterevin €405,950; Athy €340,000 — [Kfm, 18 Feb 2026](https://www.kfmradio.com/news/localnews/celbridge-house-prices-now-more-expensive-than-parts-of-capital/)
- CSO RPPI May 2026: national 12-month median €395,000; Kildare €447,876 ("second most expensive region outside Dublin"); new vs existing indices are published only quarterly at national level — [CSO RPPI May 2026](https://www.cso.ie/en/releasesandpublications/ep/p-rppi/residentialpropertypriceindexmay2026/)
- National new-dwelling prices were 6% higher in Q2 2026 than Q2 2025; annual all-dwelling growth 5.6% (lowest since Jan 2024) — [Irish Examiner](https://www.irishexaminer.com/news/arid-41898277.html)
- PPR monthly: Kildare May 2026 median €418,502 (262 full-market sales); June 2026 €405,000 (165 sales, file to 26 June); nationally new dwellings were ~25.5% of May sales — [Rightplace PPR May–June 2026](https://rightplace.ie/news/property-price-register-may-june-2026-ireland)
- A newsletter summary gives a Kildare PPR median of €405,286 across 282 transactions for Jan–Apr 2026 (single-source, treat with caution) — [BP Intelligence 2026-W17](https://bp-intelligence-newsletters.pages.dev/reports/property/2026-W17/)

- Kildare prices reported rising faster than the national trend (2026 survey) — [KildareNow](https://www.kildarenow.com/news/local-news/2047242/price-of-property-in-kildare-rising-higher-than-national-trend-survey.html)
- PPR new-build registrations 2025–26: Stradowe Meadows €365,639 (Dec 2025); Meadow Mill The Alder €325,991–€374,449 (Jul 2026); Coill an Easpaig €295,000–€357,000 (Feb–Jun 2026); An Lochán €370,044–€378,855 (Mar–May 2026); Millers Hill The Avenue €343,612 (Mar 2026); Ard na Páirce Theas €396,476 (Feb 2026); Kilwoghan Woods 46 The Drive €559,471 (Jul 2026) — sources as cited in KQ1

### Inferences
- Grossing PPR entries up by 13.5% VAT reproduces asking prices (e.g. Stradowe €365,639 × 1.135 ≈ €415,000, the Sept 2026 entry price), confirming the register understates new-build prices by about 12% relative to what buyers pay.
- Implied new-build premium over the town all-dwelling median: Athy ~€50–75k (€390–415k vs €340k); Monasterevin ~€40–70k (€445–475k vs €406k); Kildare town ~€70k (€490k vs €420k); Newbridge/Naas: new 3-bed houses (€450k+/€475k+) vs €435–440k medians; Celbridge/Maynooth/Leixlip: new 3-beds €510–655k vs €485k Celbridge median. Portlaoise/Portarlington/Killenard medians were not found, so no gap can be stated.

### Gaps
- No town-level median for NEW 3-beds in 2025–26 exists in any source read; the CSO HPM08 Eircode table and the raw PPR would need to be filtered by "new dwelling" to produce one.
- No CSO/PPR medians were found for Maynooth, Leixlip, Kilcock, Portlaoise or Portarlington.
- The MyHome €330k asking median and CSO €447,876 sales median for Kildare are inconsistent in level; the methodologies differ and neither was reconciled.
