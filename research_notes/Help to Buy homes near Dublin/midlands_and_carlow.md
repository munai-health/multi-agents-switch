# Help to Buy new-build 3-beds in the outer western / south-western commuter belt (Westmeath, Offaly, Carlow, west Laois)

Research date: 8 November 2026. Scope: Kinnegad, Mullingar, Rochfortbridge, Kilbeggan, Edenderry, Tullamore, Carlow town, Tullow, Bagenalstown, Mountmellick and Portlaoise-adjacent villages (Portlaoise itself excluded). Prices are asking prices from Daft/MyHome listings or developer/press sources, with the listing or article date noted wherever the page showed one. "Stale" flags mean the figure comes from a listing snapshot or snippet whose date could not be confirmed.

Scheme context (applies to every development below): Help to Buy (HTB) is restricted to new builds/self-builds priced at €500,000 or under, gives up to €30,000, and was extended at the current rates to 31 December 2029 with no change to the €500,000 cap in Budget 2026 — [Irish Times Budget 2026 housing coverage](https://www.irishtimes.com/ireland/housing-planning/2025/10/07/what-are-renters-landlords-and-prospective-homeowners-getting-in-the-budget); [Irish Times Budget Q&A](https://www.irishtimes.com/your-money/2025/10/06/your-budget-2026-questions-answered); [Savills on the unchanged cap](https://en.savills.pt/insight-and-opinion/savills-news/367238/help-to-buy-extension-overlooks-property-value-cap--limiting-options-for-dublin-buyers). Every development listed below is under €500,000 for its 3-bed types, so all are within the HTB price cap; whether the developer has actually registered with Revenue for HTB is stated only where a listing says so.

## Key Question 1: Development-by-development inventory, and which schemes have 3-beds under €420,000

### Takeaway
Across the four counties there are roughly 20 live new-build schemes with 3-bed houses under €500,000, and most of them sit under €420,000: the cheapest confirmed 3-beds are Fernfield Carlow (€370,000), Ballinderry Gardens Mullingar (€380,000), Fairgreen Tullow (€385,000), Abbey Park Kilbeggan (€390,000), Scotts Avenue Tullamore (€397,500), Meadows Fort Carlow (€400,000) and Chapel Park Kinnegad (€400,000–€430,000). Edenderry, Rochfortbridge and Mountmellick have no new-build 3-beds currently on sale on Daft; Glenveagh has no Carlow town scheme (its nearest is Kilkenny). Almost no listing states a completion date, and none of the Dublin-belt developers has published a 2027 phasing plan.

### Inferences
- Schemes with 3-bed houses under €420,000 as of Nov 2026: Fernfield Carlow (€370k–€380k), Ballinderry Gardens Mullingar (€380k terrace), Fairgreen Tullow (€385k–€390k), Abbey Park Kilbeggan (€390k), Scotts Avenue Tullamore (€397,500), Meadows Fort Carlow (€400k), Chapel Park Kinnegad (from €400k on the county page; €420k Oak), Royal Canal Park Mullingar (€405k), Darach Mór and Ridley Place Tullamore (€405k), The Meadows Mullingar (€414k), Carraig Mhor Carlow (€415k), plus Oak Vale Meadows Stradbally and Knocknamoe Court Abbeyleix (€375k–€395k). Ardmore Hills Mullingar starts at exactly €420,000.
- Explicit HTB/FHS registration is confirmed only for Scotts Avenue (both), Abbey Park Kilbeggan (both), Chapel Park (HTB "most homes"), Pollerton Road Carlow (HTB), Derrymore Gardens Mountrath (both) and the stale Tyrrells Brook/Derryounce snippets. All others are under the cap but silent, so buyers must confirm Revenue registration with the agent.
