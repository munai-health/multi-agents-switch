# Help to Buy-eligible new-build 3-bed houses: County Wicklow and north Wexford (Gorey)

Research date: 8 November 2026. All prices are asking prices as listed on the stated date unless marked "PPR" (Property Price Register closed sale). Help to Buy (HTB) context: the scheme's €500,000 property-value cap for new builds is unchanged and the scheme has been extended to 31 December 2029 under Budget 2026 — [RightPlace.ie](https://rightplace.ie/news/help-to-buy-scheme-ireland-2026); [Savills (criticising the unchanged cap)](https://en.savills.pt/insight-and-opinion/savills-news/367238/help-to-buy-extension-overlooks-property-value-cap--limiting-options-for-dublin-buyers); [TheJournal.ie](https://thejournal.ie/article.php?id=6501801). Most sources put the maximum refund at €30,000 (10% of price); one guide claims €35,000 for contracts from 7 Oct 2026, which I could not corroborate — [Grantsights](https://grantsights.com/blog/help-to-buy-scheme-ireland) vs [Selectra](https://selectra.ie/moving/buying/help-to-buy-schemes). HTB requires an LTV of at least 70% and can be combined with the First Home Scheme (FHS) — [Bonkers.ie](https://bonkers.ie/guides/mortgage/what-is-the-help-to-buy-scheme).

A note on PPR figures used throughout: the Property Price Register records new-dwelling prices exclusive of 13.5% VAT (the "VAT Exclusive = Yes" flag). I computed medians from the official CSV downloads for 2025 and 2026 (Wicklow and Wexford) and show both the raw ex-VAT figure and the ×1.135 VAT-inclusive equivalent, which is what a buyer actually pays and what HTB measures against. The PPR has no bedroom count, so scheme-level PPR medians mix 2-, 3- and 4-beds. Source: [Property Price Register CSV downloads](https://www.propertypriceregister.ie/) (files PPR-2025-Wicklow, PPR-2026-Wicklow, PPR-2025-Wexford, PPR-2026-Wexford, downloaded 8 Nov 2026).

## Key Question 1: Development-by-development inventory — which schemes have 3-bed HOUSES at or under €500,000, and which are best value?

### Takeaway
Only a handful of schemes in the study area currently offer a 3-bed *house* at or under the €500,000 HTB cap: in Wicklow the open-market options are Ballycomyn (from €460,000) and Torcaill Wood (3-bed terrace €480,000) in Blessington, Mariner's Point (The Holland, €495,000) in Wicklow town, Lorrin Lodge (€499,000) in Rathnew, Rosebay at Altidore Gardens (€500,000, Newtownmountkennedy), Causeway Meadows (3-bed semi €485,000, Roundwood) and Aldborough Manor in Baltinglass (€325,000–€355,000). In north Wexford, Gorey is far cheaper: Pearsons Brook (€400,000–€415,000), Churchfields (€410,000) and Deerpark (Phase 5, 3-bed POA but PPR closings around €345,000–€485,000). Greystones, Delgany, Bray, Kilcoole and Ashford have nothing new under €500,000 for a 3-bed house; Arklow and Rathdrum have no active new-build scheme at all.

### Inferences
- For a 3-bed buyer, the only Wicklow affordable-purchase three-beds with a 2026 window were Ballycomyn (12 units, min €385k) and Market Close Dunlavin (3 units); both windows have closed. Lott Lane Kilcoole (24 three-beds) is the most significant 2027 opportunity on the DART-adjacent side of the county, but prices and launch date are unconfirmed and the council's own 2026 affordable target is only 26 units.
- Affordable purchase prices (e.g. €385k at Ballycomyn vs €460k open-market Cairn 3-beds; €290k at Rams Brooke vs €400k–€415k at Pearsons Brook) are 15–30% below open market and are HTB-compatible, making them the best value route if a buyer qualifies under the income limits.
