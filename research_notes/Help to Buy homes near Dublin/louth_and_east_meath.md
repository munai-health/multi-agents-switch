# Help to Buy–eligible new-build 3-bed houses: County Louth and coastal/east Meath (as of 8 November 2026)

Scope note: Research was done on 8 Nov 2026 using daft.ie / myhome.ie / property.ie new-homes listings, developer and agent pages, Louth County Council pages, Irish Rail/NTA/TFI, RTÉ and local press. Several developer sites (newtownwood.ie, linenfield.ie, reagunneproperty.ie) returned HTTP 503 and the Dundalk Democrat / Irish Times returned 403, so some items rely on portal listings and search snippets. The shared web-search budget was exhausted before every gap could be closed; gaps are flagged below. Listing dates are stated where the portal showed them; anything older than 2026 is flagged as possibly stale.

Scheme framing (national rules that apply to every development below):
- Help to Buy (HTB): refund of up to €30,000 (max 10% of price) on a NEW build or self-build costing €500,000 or less; extended to 31 December 2029 by Budget 2025 — [Chartered Accountants Ireland](https://charteredaccountants.ie/News/help-to-buy-guidance-updated-for-scheme-extension); [Sherry FitzGerald Budget 2025 note](https://www.sherryfitz.ie/news/Budget-2025); [Revenue HTB overview (published 1 Jan 2026)](https://www.revenue.ie/en/property/help-to-buy-incentive/index.aspx)
- First Home Scheme (FHS) price ceilings: County Louth €425,000 (house and apartment); County Meath €475,000 — [First Home Scheme price ceilings](https://www.firsthomescheme.ie/about-the-scheme/property-price-ceilings/). Implication: several Louth 3-beds priced €437,500–€455,000 are HTB-eligible but above the Louth FHS ceiling.

## Key Question 1: Which schemes in these towns have 3-bed houses under €450,000, and which are cheapest per square metre?

### Takeaway
As of 8 Nov 2026 the cheapest open-market new 3-bed houses are Ushers Mill, Drogheda (from €380,000), Ballymakenny Park 3-bed terraces (€395,000, 92 m², new release 7 Nov 2026), Hearthfield Dundalk (from €399,990), Linenfield Phase 2 (€405,000–€410,000) and Gort Mell (c. €410,000–€425,000, dates uncertain); on a per-m² basis Gort Mell (c. €3,628/m²), Hearthfield B1 (€3,684/m²), Larrix Grove Type A (€3,692/m², but €480k) and Fitzgerald Gardens (c. €3,863/m²) are the best value, while Ballymakenny Park's €395k terrace is the cheapest headline price but the most expensive per m² (€4,293/m²). Nothing new-build is currently listed in Bettystown, Laytown, Mornington, Julianstown, Stamullen or Gormanston.
