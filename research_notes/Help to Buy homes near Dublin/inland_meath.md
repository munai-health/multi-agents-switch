# Inland County Meath: new-build 3-bed houses eligible for Help to Buy (≤€500,000), for sale / launching / completing by end-2027

Research date: 8 November 2026. Prices are asking prices as shown on the portal or newspaper on the date given in brackets. "Daft Nov 2026" = the live daft.ie County Meath new-homes index pages fetched 8 Nov 2026. Where the only source is an undated or older listing I flag it as POSSIBLY STALE. Note: several portal listings carry an original "listed" date (e.g. 2024) but show prices that have evidently been updated since; treat the price as current-ish but verify with the agent.

Scope note: Help to Buy requires a new build at ≤€500,000; the First Home Scheme (FHS) has its own local price ceilings. Portal listings only occasionally state HTB/FHS eligibility explicitly; where they do, it is recorded below. Where not stated, "not stated" is recorded rather than inferred.

---

## Key Question 1: Development-by-development inventory, and which schemes have 3-beds under €450,000

### Takeaway
As of November 2026, the sub-€450k new 3-bed house in inland Meath is largely confined to the western and northern towns (Athboy, Kells, Oldcastle, Rathmoylon, Longwood, Ballivor) plus Navan terraces/duplexes (Sommerville Bluebell 2 at €435k, Limebrook terrace at €440k, Beaufort 3-bed duplex at €412.5k, The Willows Dunshaughlin 3-bed duplex at €437.5k). The M3/Dublin-fringe towns (Ashbourne, Ratoath, Dunboyne) now start at €475k–€500k for a 3-bed and sit at or over the HTB ceiling. Milestone (Ashbourne) is confirmed only as "launching next year" via Grimes with a 1/2/3/4-bed mix; developer and pricing are unconfirmed.

## Key Question 6: Meath County Council affordable purchase launches of 3-beds in 2026/2027

### Takeaway
Confirmed council affordable-purchase activity is in Navan (Robinrath, opened 27 Nov 2025, 3-beds €360k–€365k vs €435k market value; Beaufort Phase 3, Sept 2025) and Kells (Foxwood Park, 3-bed terrace €322,500; 2-bed starter homes from €292,500, listed Jan 2026, phase 1 sold out), plus the LDA's 50-home Baker Hall, Navan. No specific 2026/2027 3-bed affordable launch in Trim, Ashbourne, Dunshaughlin or elsewhere was found; the council's page reportedly states no scheme is currently open (date unclear) and the council's own pages returned 503 on fetch.
